Banana Bread

yield: loaf pan

0.5 cup butter
1 cup sugar
2 egg
3 ripe bananas
0.5 cup yogurt
1.25 cup all-purpose flour
1 cup whole wheat flour
1 teaspoon baking powder
1 teaspoon baking soda
0.75 teaspoon salt
Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit (177 degrees Celsius).
Cream butter and sugar.
Beat in eggs.
Add bananas and yogurt.
Whisk dries together. Fold dries into wets.
Bake forty-five to fifty-five minutes. It is ready when a knife comes out mostly dry.
